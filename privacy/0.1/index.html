Privacy

Version 0.1, published 17 October 2026 · applies to the MatchTempo beta for Apple Watch, iPhone and matchtempo.app

In short:

  • Your groups see your name, replies, goals and results.
  • Your health data stays on your iPhone and Watch.
  • Your account is kept in the UK. Delete it any time.
  • MatchTempo is for people aged 18 and over.

Nothing is sold, and nothing is used for advertising or tracking. This is a beta draft and has not yet had a legal review.

Who we are

MatchTempo is made by an independent developer in the United Kingdom, who is responsible for your data under UK data protection law. We are the controller of the data described here. Contact us at support at matchtempo dot app.

Two ways to use MatchTempo

Only recording on your Watch

You don't have to sign in to record your own games. If you don't, everything stays on your Apple Watch and iPhone and we hold nothing about you on our servers.

Signed in, for groups and games

To start or join a group you sign in with Apple or Google. We then keep your account and your groups' games on our servers, so everyone in the group sees the same teams and results. Your recordings stay on your devices; only the game details below are shared.

What stays on your devices

We never see these. They stay on your Apple Watch and iPhone, and in Apple Health if you save workouts there:

  • heart rate, distance, calories, your route and location during a game, motion, recovery, effort and enjoyment;
  • your date of birth and gender, if you add them;
  • your recordings.

Recordings move from your Watch to your iPhone using Apple's own connection between the two devices.

Apple Health

With your permission, MatchTempo reads heart-rate samples, workouts and active energy from Apple Health to show your figures. If you turn on Save to Health, it writes the workout, with its route, active energy and distance, to Apple Health. You can change these permissions at any time in the Health app or in Settings. Health data is never used for advertising, never sold and never shared with anyone else.

Location, motion and Bluetooth

Location is used only while you're recording, to measure distance and save your route. Motion data helps describe your movement during a recording. Bluetooth is used only to connect a heart-rate strap you choose.

What we keep when you sign in

Your account

  • Your first and last name from Apple or Google, a nickname if you add one, and which name your groups see.
  • Your sign-in details: our sign-in service keeps the email address Apple or Google gives us (with Apple, this can be a private relay address) and an identifier for your Apple or Google account. We use these only to sign you in and never show them to your groups.
  • That you confirmed you are 18 or over, and how: through Apple's age check on your iPhone, or by ticking the box. We don't store your age or date of birth.
  • Your agreement to the Terms and this policy: which versions, the date and time, whether you agreed in the app or a browser, and the app or site version. A new record is added each time you agree.
  • Whether you have chosen to be left out of stats and tables.

Your groups and games

  • Groups: name, organisers, members and settings.
  • Players: first name, last name, nickname, squad number, usual position, stronger foot and preferred side. An organiser may add players who don't use the app.
  • Games: dates, kick-off times, venues (name, address and map location), kits, teams, results, scorers, own goals and Player of the Match.
  • Replies to games, and goals you send to the organiser to check. A goal carries the goal and the score, never your health data.
  • A history of changes, so a corrected result keeps the earlier one and the reason.

Kick-off weather

If a venue has a map location, our server asks Apple Weather for the forecast at kick-off. Only the location, rounded to about 1 km, is sent to Apple, with no names or other details. We keep the forecast for at most 12 hours and never store it with results.

Logs and security

When the app or a browser talks to our servers, our hosting provider records technical logs, such as IP address, time and the request made. These are used to keep the service running and secure, and are deleted after a short period.

Who sees what

  • Your groups: organisers and members see players' names, squad numbers, replies, teams, goals, results and the table. Last names are shown to organisers only.
  • Anyone with a game link: an organiser can share a link to a game. Anyone holding it can see the players' names, the teams, the score, the scorers, Player of the Match and the kick-off weather, without signing in. Links stop working 30 days after kick-off, and the organiser can turn a link off sooner. To have a link removed, ask your organiser or email us.
  • Stats and tables: if you turn off "Show me in stats and tables", you are left out of the table and stats for all your groups. You still appear in teams and as a scorer.
  • What you share yourself: when you share teams or results through the iPhone share sheet, they go to the app you pick, such as WhatsApp or Messages, and that app's privacy policy applies.

Why we use your data

  • To run MatchTempo for you (your account, groups, games and tables): because it is needed for our agreement with you.
  • Players added by an organiser, game links, logs and security: because we and the organiser have a legitimate interest in running the group's games and keeping MatchTempo safe, and this has little effect on the people involved.
  • Your agreement records: to show what you agreed to and when.

We don't use your data for advertising, we don't sell it, and we don't send marketing.

Who helps us

These companies process data for us under contract, only to provide their service:

  • Supabase hosts our database and sign-in service. Your data is stored in London. Supabase's support staff may be able to access it from outside the UK, under the safeguards UK data protection law requires.
  • Apple and Google run Sign in with Apple and Google sign-in. Apple also provides the weather forecasts and TestFlight.
  • Our payment provider will take payments for Group Pro after the beta. Nothing is charged during the beta.
  • Apple iCloud Mail handles email sent to our support address.

Testing through TestFlight

During the beta, Apple's TestFlight may share crash reports, basic device information (such as model and software version) and any feedback or screenshots you choose to send with us. Apple's privacy policy covers how Apple handles this.

Backups

Your recordings are stored on your iPhone and, unless you turn this off in Settings, included in your iPhone's iCloud or computer backups. Those backups are handled by Apple under your own Apple account, not by us.

How long we keep data

  • Your account: until you delete it. You can delete it at any time in the app, in You. This removes your account, your sign-in details and your agreement records.
  • Your groups' history: results stay with the group after you leave or delete your account, so the table still adds up. Your player entry stays with the name the organiser entered, but it is no longer linked to you. If you are the only organiser, the app asks you to hand the group over first, or archives it.
  • Game links: 30 days after kick-off.
  • Weather forecasts: at most 12 hours.
  • On your devices: until you delete the recording or the app. Deleting a recording in MatchTempo never removes the workout from Apple Health.

Age

MatchTempo is for people aged 18 and over. If you tell us you're under 18, or Apple's age check says so, sign-up stops and nothing is sent to our servers. If you think someone under 18 has an account, please email us and we'll delete it.

Cookies

The app and matchtempo.app set no cookies and use no analytics or tracking.

Your rights

You have rights under UK data protection law, including to get a copy of your data, to correct it, to have it deleted, and to object to how we use it. You can do much of this yourself in the app. For anything else, or for a copy of your data, email support at matchtempo dot app. If an organiser added you to a group and you don't use the app, you can email us too.

If you're unhappy with how we've handled your data, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes to this policy

Each version of this policy has a number and a date, shown at the top of this page. For a big change, the app will tell you what has changed and ask you to agree again the next time you open Play, before anything new is collected. Small changes, such as clearer wording, only update the date and version.
