Email support at matchtempo dot app and we'll reply as soon as we can.

If you're testing the beta, the quickest way to report a problem is from TestFlight: take a screenshot in MatchTempo and tap Share Beta Feedback, or open TestFlight and tap Send Beta Feedback.

Common questions

What do I need?

An iPhone on iOS 17 or later. To record games you also need an Apple Watch on watchOS 10 or later. A chest heart-rate strap is optional.

How do I join the beta?

Install Apple's TestFlight app, then open the invite link from matchtempo.app/get or the one your organiser sent you. The Watch app installs from the Watch app on your iPhone.

Do I need the phone with me during a game?

No. The Watch records on its own and sends the recording to your iPhone when they're back together.

Why does the distance differ from other apps?

Distance on a small pitch is hard to measure with GPS, and each app estimates it slightly differently. MatchTempo's figure is an estimate and we're still checking it against real games.

Do my players need the app?

No. Teams and results go to your group chat as plain text that anyone can read. Replying and voting from a link in any phone's browser is planned.

Does it cost anything?

Not during the beta. After launch, running your game stays free. Group Pro will add paid extras for organisers. Taking payments for games will be free to set up, and players see exactly what they'll pay before they confirm. Players don't need it to take part.

How do I delete my data?

If you signed in, delete your account in the app, in You. To remove what the app stored on your devices, delete it from your iPhone and Watch. Workouts saved to Apple Health stay there until you delete them in the Health app. See Privacy for more.
