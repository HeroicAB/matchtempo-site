Who can use MatchTempo

  • To start or join a group you need to sign in with Apple or Google. You can record your own games on your Watch without signing in.
  • We use the email address that comes with your Apple or Google sign-in, which with Apple can be a private relay address. You don't type one in, and you can't change it in MatchTempo.
  • Keep your Apple or Google account secure. Anything done after signing in with it is treated as done by you.

  • we may reset or move server data if we have to, and will tell you first where we can;

Recordings are kept on your iPhone and Watch, not by us, so keep your iPhone backed up if they matter to you.

Managers and assistant managers

  • Each group has one manager, usually the person who started it. The manager can hand the group over to another player.
  • The manager can make up to 3 players assistant managers. An assistant manager must be a player in the group who has signed in. Assistant managers are part of Group Pro, which is free during the beta.
  • Assistant managers help run games: they can schedule and cancel games, record replies, move people in and out of places, add guests, pick teams, record results, scorers and Player of the Match, check goals, make a game void and share a game link.
  • Only the manager can add, change or remove players, handle join links, change the group's settings (such as points, seasons and reply rules), and add or remove assistant managers.
  • An assistant manager stops being one if the manager removes them, if they step down, or if they leave the group or stop being linked to their player.
  • If you agree to be an assistant manager, the parts of these terms that apply to managers apply to you for what you do.

Groups and what other people see

  • When you join a group, its members see your name (or the name you choose to show), your squad number, whether you have a place in a game, the teams you play in, your goals and results. They also see who the manager and assistant managers are.
  • The manager and any assistant managers also see your replies and when you made them. The manager also sees your full name.
  • The manager can add players who don't use the app, and the manager or an assistant manager can add guests for one game. If you add someone, you must have a good reason to and should let them know. Only add the details the game needs.
  • Managers and assistant managers are responsible for keeping teams, results and the table accurate. A correction to a result is kept with the reason given.
  • Goals you send to the manager carry the goal and the score. They never carry your health data.
  • Player of the Match votes are secret. Vote honestly, and only for someone who played.
  • Keep reasons and notes short and factual. Don't put health details, such as an injury, or anything unkind about someone in a reason or note: members can read the reason a game was made void.

Replies, places and the waiting list

  • When you reply I'm in, the group's rule decides whether you have a place or are on the waiting list. If someone drops out, the first person waiting moves up straight away, so keep your reply up to date.
  • Your manager may set a time after which you can't drop out yourself in the app (24 hours before kick-off unless they change it). After that, tell your manager or an assistant manager, who can record it for you.
  • The manager or an assistant manager can always move someone in or onto the waiting list. How places are shared out is up to the group, not us.

Join links

The manager can share a link that lets people join the group. Anyone who signs in and opens it sees the group's name and its players' shown names, so share it only with people you expect to join, and turn it off when you no longer need it. Only pick the player who is you. If you pick the wrong one, ask your manager to fix it.

Game links

The manager or an assistant manager can share a link to a game. Anyone holding the link can see, without signing in, the group's name, the date and kick-off time, the venue with its address and map location, the teams and the players' shown names, the score, the scorers, Player of the Match, the kick-off weather and whether the game was made void. Links stop working 30 days after kick-off, and the manager or an assistant manager can turn a link off sooner. If you want a link removed, ask your manager or email us.

If you reply to a game from a link without signing in, you agree to these terms and our Privacy policy for that reply.

Health and fitness

MatchTempo measures things like heart rate, distance, calories and recovery to help you understand your games. These figures are estimates. MatchTempo is not a medical device and doesn't give medical advice. Football carries a risk of injury: play within your limits, and speak to a doctor if you have a health condition or feel unwell.

Your health data stays on your iPhone and Watch, and in Apple Health if you save workouts there. We never see it.

Kick-off weather

Forecasts in MatchTempo come from Apple Weather. Weather data may not be accurate, and forecasts can change. Don't rely on them for safety decisions, and check conditions yourself before you play. Apple Weather data sources.
