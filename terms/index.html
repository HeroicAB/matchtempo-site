Game links

An organiser can share a link to a game. Anyone holding the link can see the players' names, the teams, the score, the scorers, Player of the Match and the kick-off weather, without signing in. Links stop working 30 days after kick-off, and the organiser can turn a link off sooner. If you want a link removed, ask your organiser or email us.

If you reply to a game from a link without signing in, you agree to these terms and our Privacy policy for that reply.
